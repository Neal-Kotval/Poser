## Colors
Dark photo canvas (`Palette.background` `#090A0E`), grouped system surfaces (`Palette.surface`, `Palette.elevated`), white-at-12% borders, monochrome white/gray text hierarchy (`#FFFFFF`, `#ADADAD`, `#7A7A7A`). Full table in `.impeccable/design-full.md` and `Poser/Design.swift`.

## Typography
`PoserType` semantic SwiftUI roles (SF Rounded for wordmark/display/titles, SF Pro for body/supporting/captions) with Dynamic Type support. Full guidance in `.impeccable/design-full.md`.

## Components
`PrimaryButton`, `SecondaryButton`, `poserGlass` (native Liquid Glass with Reduce Transparency fallback), `PhotoTagPills`, `SectionLabel`, `PoserSurface`, floating bottom glass nav, Home `For you | Saved`, and Profile `Shots | Poses | Progress`. Full specs in `.impeccable/design-full.md`.
